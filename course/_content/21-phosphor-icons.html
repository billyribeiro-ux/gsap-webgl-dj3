Phosphor is a flexible, consistent icon family — and because every icon is real SVG, you can do far more than recolor it. You can animate its paths, swap its weight (thin → regular → bold → fill → duotone) on interaction, and choreograph tiny moments of delight. In your SvelteKit app, phosphor-svelte exposes all 9,000+ as tree-shakeable components.

Installing & using Phosphor in Svelte

pnpm add phosphor-svelte, then import only the icons you use:

  • import {`{ Heart, BellRinging }`} from 'phosphor-svelte'; — tree-shaken, so your bundle only ships the icons you reference.
  • Every icon takes size, color, weight and mirrored props. Drive weight from $state and you get an instant outline-to-fill toggle, like the heart.
  • Because the icon is an <svg>, you target it from your scoped styles with :global(svg) and animate it — rotate, draw-on, pulse — exactly like the bell and sun here.
Make icon motion meaningful. A bell should ring when something arrives, not at random. A heart should pop on your action. The best icon animations confirm a result or signal a state change — they're feedback, not fireworks. And as always: respect prefers-reduced-motion; a static icon is never broken, a spinning one can be nauseating.

Combine with the draw-on technique

Remember stroke draw-on from the SVG lesson? Apply it to an outline Phosphor icon and it paints itself into view — perfect for a checkmark confirming success, or a logo assembling on load. Phosphor's clean, single-path outlines are ideal subjects. Icons are where everything you've learned about SVG and CSS animation pays off at the smallest, most-used scale in your interface.
